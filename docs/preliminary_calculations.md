# Preliminary calculations

## Takt allocation

One 5 s cycle is allocated as: confirmation 0.4 s, separation 0.7 s, transfer 1.0 s, settle 0.4 s, image/laser acquisition 1.0 s, judgement 0.3 s, eject/return 1.2 s.

## Cylinder sizing

For a 0.12 kg part on a guide with assumed friction coefficient 0.2 and 0.5 m/s² acceleration, required force with factor 3 is below 1 N. A 16 mm bore cylinder at 0.5 MPa provides about 100 N theoretical force; a 20 mm transfer cylinder provides about 157 N. This leaves margin for seals, guide friction and occasional misalignment.

## Sensor layout

Five photoelectric checks cover bowl exit, track queue, first gate, second gate and V-block arrival. Laser micrometer measures the critical diameter; the top camera checks overall contour, step position, orientation and surface-defect candidates.
