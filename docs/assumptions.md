# Assumptions

- The workpiece dimensions, mass and 12 parts/minute target follow the resume project definition.
- Vibratory bowl tuning, feeder-controller waveform and final track clearances require physical parts; the CAD shows the complete mechanical route and adjustment interfaces.
- Camera, telecentric lens, laser micrometer, cylinders, sensors and valve island are envelope models.
- AI defect classes are scratches, dents, missing material and gross contour defects; no accuracy is claimed without a labelled dataset.
